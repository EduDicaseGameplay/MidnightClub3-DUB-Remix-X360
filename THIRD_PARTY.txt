Third party work used in this port

1. MidnightClub3-Port (https://github.com/christiand0797/MidnightClub3-Port), MIT License, Copyright (c) 2026 TGP482.
   The Windows port of the same game, used as the reference for many fixes. x360/runtime/game/mc3_timing.cpp
   (fixed step clock and replays), mc3_career.cpp (career events), mc3_lod.cpp (level of detail) and mc3_world.cpp
   (traffic density) are adapted from its templates/game/src/game files. Parts of x360/runtime/gpu (the depth copy,
   signed blends, masked clears, visibility tests), x360/runtime/xdk/d3d8.cpp (pushed vertex constants),
   x360/runtime/xdk/dsound.cpp and x360/runtime/kernel/files.cpp follow its runtime. Several function addresses in
   pc-recomp/config/game.toml come from it and are marked there.

2. mc3port (SickSartori), MIT License.
   The WMA music decoder fix in x360/runtime/game/mc3.cpp (an overlap-add loop with a block count of 0) follows the
   cause it found. The bytes were checked again in this XBE. In x360/runtime/xdk/dsound.cpp, the routing of a voice
   through its mix-in buffer (SetOutputBuffer: the mix-in's volume, bins and pitch, its 10 dB level and the stereo to
   mono half), a voice made without mix bins taking them from its first SetMixBinVolumes, and a stream stopped by an
   asynchronous FlushEx handing packets back until Pause follow its src/pc/audio_gains.h and src/pc/dsound_hle.cpp,
   written again for this runtime.

3. JSRF Unleashed (https://github.com/JuMatt/JetSetRadioFuture-Unleashed), MIT License, Copyright (c) 2026 sp00nz.
   The runtime comes from my Jet Set Radio Future port, where the idea of running one game thread at a time
   (x360/runtime/kernel/guestlock.cpp) came from this project.

4. XboxXBE-Recomp (https://github.com/TGP482/XboxXBE-Recomp) and BloodyRoarExtremeRecomp
   (https://github.com/bromurok/BloodyRoarExtremeRecomp), MIT License.
   The recompiler in pc-recomp/sdk and the base of the Xbox 360 runtime. Licenses in pc-recomp.

5. Zydis and toml++, downloaded by CMake when the PC tools are built (x360/tools/CMakeLists.txt), MIT License.
